# Duri Duri · Synbiotic Fiber

A one-page, scroll-driven web story for Duri Duri Synbiotic Fiber (portrait 9:16, 13 scenes).

- Swipe up, scroll down, or press ↓ to go forward. Swipe down, scroll up, or press ↑ to go back.
- Everything is in `index.html`: product photos, fonts and illustrations are embedded, so there are no other files.
- Open `index.html#5` to jump straight to scene 5.

## Scenes
1. Hero: both flavours
2. Green-grape box opens and the ingredients pop out
3. Resource: the 4 ingredients
4. Durian peel → POS
5. Durian seed → Resistant Starch (R2)
6. Probiotic → Bacillus subtilis DE111
7. Ginger → 6-Gingerol + 6-Shogaol
8. Upcycling: the ingredients swirl into powder
9. Powder fills the green-grape sachet
10. The sachet is sealed
11. The lemon sachet slides out: 2 flavours
12. Each sachet goes into its own box: 2 pack sizes
13. The boxes close and the scene returns to the hero
